Here is what your new watchman can do.

What it does
01
Capture in seconds
Paste a link, drop a screenshot, or forward a posting from your phone through the Telegram bot. Argus reads the details (role, organisation, deadline, requirements) and files them for you.
02
Never miss a deadline
Every opportunity lands on a timeline with reminders, a daily digest at the hour you choose, and a weekly application goal that keeps a streak going. Deadlines can go straight to your calendar.
03
Apply in your voice
Give Argus your CV once, several versions if you have them, and it drafts each application around your real experience, matching the right CV to the right role.
04
New openings, found for you
Once a day Argus goes hunting: it scans for jobs, scholarships and programmes that fit what you told it you’re after, and brings back the ones worth your time.
05
Yours, on your machine
Everything you track lives on your own computer, never on a server you don’t control. During the beta Argus sends anonymous usage counts so we can improve it, and never the contents of anything you track. Settings → Export hands you all your data any time.
